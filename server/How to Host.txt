HOW TO HOST DISCORD LITE ON YOUR PC
=====================================

Right now Discord Lite is hosted on whoever runs it as the "host".
If you want to host on YOUR computer instead, do this:

1) Download the installer (recommended):
   https://github.com/AndrewRaney/Discord-app/releases/latest

   Get:  Discord-Lite-Setup-….exe
   (Prefer Setup over Portable — Setup can auto-update later.)

2) Run the installer and open Discord Lite.

3) On the login screen, leave "Server address" BLANK.
   Create an account (or sign in). You are now the host.
   The app runs on your PC and opens a public tunnel link.

4) In the app go to:
   Settings → Connection
   Copy the "Public tunnel" link.

5) Send that link to your friends.
   Friends paste it into "Server address", then Create Account
   on YOUR server (not their own blank/local one).

NOTES
- Keep Discord Lite open while friends are using it.
- Your PC needs to stay on and connected to the internet.
- Only one person should host at a time for a shared group of friends.
- Friends who already have accounts on someone else's host need new
  accounts on yours (each host has its own user list / chat history).
